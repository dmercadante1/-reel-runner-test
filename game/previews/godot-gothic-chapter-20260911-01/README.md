# MAN WITH A MOVIE CAMERA — Gothic Horror chapter study

A first playable 1080p chapter with nine rooms across the Courtyard, Catacombs, Cathedral, Castle and Dracula’s chamber. Artwork and animation remain open for review.

Arrows or A/D move; Up/W jumps; Space/C films; R reloads one spare reel; X fires an earned Super Shot; Down/S drops through small platforms. Use the touch buttons on iPhone Safari. Full screen provides a screen-filling fallback when native fullscreen is unavailable.

Evade the warning, then film the enemy’s recovery. Gold reels refill spare reserves; silver reels grant Super Shots. Dracula must be exposed for a Super Shot to affect him. Retry restores room-entry supplies. Progress currently saves the room number, not the full inventory.

Earlier previews and R5 are preserved. Desktop browser tests do not establish physical iPhone performance or audible sound quality.
