Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-11 for this original Gothic game. No Castlevania source code or assets incorporated. Existing authorized cameraman artwork is reused without changing identity or costume.

- trees.png: transparent wide moonlit fir/bare-tree and iron-fence layer; naturalistic painted pixel-art inspiration, cool navy palette, no castle. Prompt requested 1536×1024 and seamless horizontal scenery; returned 1536×1024 RGBA. Layer repeats in the distance; not certified perfectly seam-matched.
- enemies.png: skeleton and werewolf atlas, 8 columns × 2 rows: four patrol steps, face block, anticipation, slash, exposed recoil. Prompt requested 3072×1024; actual returned 2172×724 RGBA. Runtime slices actual dimensions, uses consistent relative foot anchors. First/fourth patrol steps are closely related; additional animation polish remains subject to review.
- objects.png: bronze reel, silver reel, garnet heart, iron key, fountain, ivy pillar; naturalistically painted with worn materials and moonlight, transparent. Returned 1536×1024 RGBA. Independently measured source regions avoid treating the sheet as six exact equal cells. Reels show wound film and openings rather than plain circles.
