## Controls and progression

PRESS START or Enter. Arrows/A-D move; Up/W jumps; hold Space/C to film; X/Shift flashes; P pauses; R restarts. Phone landscape has movement, jump, FILM and FLASH controls. Capture the three Courtyard creatures, then reach its far gate to enter the Cathedral.
